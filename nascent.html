01 / THE STORY

I spent 14 years running Arquidromo, designing, building, and selling real estate in Mexico. Co-founded it in 2011, ran it until last year, got featured in international publications along the way. But I'm more interested in where AI takes the world than where design takes it.

La-La Land started as a SaaS platform for Mexican residential developers. Shipped interactive lot maps and Google-like street view. Full stack built solo, shipped in November. Three developers signed up, paying us in three different shapes. One on monthly SaaS. One on a 1M MXN annual content deal, plus 1% on any lots that dev sells through us (slow cycle, pipeline warming but no closes yet). One paid 30K MXN setup, flew me out twice on his dime, then ghosted on the subscription. The willingness to pay is clearly there. The SaaS shape isn't.

That taught me: the money in this market isn't in software. It's in the 3% commission changing hands on every closed lot. And right now Mexican developers are running Instagram ads offering that 3% to any broker willing to sell. It's a frenzy. They're not picky. They just need to move inventory. It's a market tailwind you can verify yourself.

I'm now rebuilding La-La Land as the AI-native agency for Mexican residential real estate, in a territory where I have relationships, tooling, and 14-16 hour days of conviction. The exact product shape is something I'm iterating toward, not something I claim to have solved. Pre-seed is when you bet on the person, the territory, and the direction.
